Teach to Learn

Teaching materials, learning resources, and tools for exploring language.

This page brings together materials for teaching, learning, and exploring language. It includes teaching notes, language-learning resources, and materials developed through my teaching and community-based documentation work, for audiences from students encountering linguistics for the first time to teachers looking for materials to adapt.

Teaching Materials

Language, Society, and the Individual

Assignments developed for LIN 207 Language, Society, and the Individual, University at Buffalo, Fall 2026. Each includes step-by-step instructions and grading criteria and can be adapted for other courses.

Draw-A-Map Task

A perceptual dialectology activity. Students and a consultant each draw where they believe people speak differently on a blank map, label and explain each area with a linguistic example, then compare the two maps in a short reflection on language variation.

Learning goal: to explore how people perceive linguistic variation across geographical regions.

For students and instructors in sociolinguistics or introductory linguistics · PDF, 2 pages · Fall 2026

New Expressions

Students write six mini-dictionary entries, three for slang expressions found online and three for expressions they create, analyzing origin, word formation, and use in context, followed by a reflection on language change, word formation, and identity. Includes an entry template.

Learning goal: to explore how new expressions emerge, how speakers create language, and why some expressions spread while others never catch on. Entries are compiled into a class mini-dictionary; students may opt out without affecting their grade.

For students and instructors in sociolinguistics, morphology, or introductory linguistics · PDF, 5 pages · Fall 2026

Languages in Eastern Asia

Guest lecture slides for LIN 106 Languages of the World (instructor: Gladys Camacho-Rios), University at Buffalo, October 15, 2025. For students and instructors in introductory linguistics or typology courses · PDF, 72 slides · October 2025

Responsibly Using Generative AI Tools for Literature Searches

Guidelines for students on using LLM-based tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini to find literature: what they may and may not be used for, rules for prompting and verifying every citation, the tools' limitations, a workflow, and six prompt templates. Developed for LIN 207, Fall 2026.

Community

Community-based Language Work

Linguistics in Anyuan Hakka

In 2024, I developed an introductory course of general linguistics and taught it in Anyuan Hakka to a group of community members who have no prior linguistics background. The course provided easy access to linguistic knowledge for local community members who do not speak English (or Mandarin) and equipped them with basic linguistic tools to better understand Anyuan Hakka, their own everyday language.

For Anyuan Hakka speakers · Taught in 2024

AYHAK · Anyuan Hakka Documentation Project

Online meetups for people from Anyuan to keep speaking Anyuan Hakka, an edible-plant photo book with local names and audio (in development), and other community resources for the language.
