Teaching Materials

Draw-A-Map Task

A perceptual dialectology activity: students and a consultant each draw where they believe people speak differently on a blank map, label and explain each area with a linguistic example, then compare the two maps in a short reflection on language variation.

For students and instructors in sociolinguistics or introductory linguistics · PDF, 2 pages · Available · Fall 2026 · Use: adapt for your course

New Expressions

Students write six mini-dictionary entries, three for slang expressions found online and three for expressions they create, analyzing origin, word formation, and use in context, followed by a reflection on language change, word formation, and identity.

For students and instructors in sociolinguistics, morphology, or introductory linguistics · PDF, 5 pages · Available · Fall 2026 · Use: adapt for your course; requires prior knowledge of word formation and language change

Languages in Eastern Asia

Guest lecture slides covering language families of Eastern Asia, languages in China and Chinese (Sinitic) languages, Chinese dialects, and a case study of Anyuan Hakka and the AYHAK documentation project.

About this version: these are the slides as presented in October 2025. Some slides rely on audio, video, and in-class explanation; the overview of language families and Chinese dialect groups (slides 3–24) reads best on its own. Anyuan Hakka transcriptions in the slides predate the current AYHAK conventions; see AYHAK for current forms.

For students and instructors in introductory linguistics or typology courses · PDF, 72 slides · Available · October 2025 · Use: requires instructor context

Writing systems of Chinese languages: intelligibility, literacy, and language policy in Mainland China, Hong Kong and Macao, and Taiwan.

Learning Resources

East Asian Languages in Music and Film

A listening and viewing list: songs and films in Tibetan, Mandarin, Hakka, a Taiwanese Indigenous language, Taiwan Mandarin, Jin, Cantonese, and Shanghai Wu.

For anyone curious about the languages of Eastern Asia · Web page · Available · October 2026 · Use: on your own or in class

Anyuan Hakka

Resources from AYHAK, the Anyuan Hakka Documentation Project, including online meetups for people from Anyuan to keep speaking Anyuan Hakka and an edible-plant photo book with local names and audio (Plants of Anyuan, in progress).

  • 实用安远话五百句 · 500 Everyday Sentences: find a sentence, listen to the recording, and say it after the speaker. Web page with audio · In progress: sentences available, recordings being added · Use: on your own
  • 安远话词典 · Anyuan Hakka Dictionary: a community dictionary with Chinese characters, IPA, definitions, example sentences, and recordings, with each form labeled by the town or village it comes from. Web page with audio · In progress: searchable now, entries and recordings being added · Use: on your own
  • Speaking Anyuan Hakka 安远话入门十讲: an Anyuan Hakka course taught in Mandarin, for speakers and non-speakers alike. Online course · Planned
  • Linguistics in Anyuan Hakka: In 2024, I developed an introductory course in general linguistics and taught it in Anyuan Hakka to a group of community members who had no prior linguistics background. The course provided easy access to linguistic knowledge for local community members who do not speak English (or Mandarin) and equipped them with basic linguistic tools to better understand Anyuan Hakka, their own everyday language. For Anyuan Hakka speakers · Taught in 2024

Responsibly Using Generative AI Tools for Literature Searches

Guidelines for students on using LLM-based tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini to find literature: what they may and may not be used for, rules for prompting and verifying every citation, the tools' limitations, a workflow, and six prompt templates.

For students and instructors · PDF, 5 pages · Available · Version 1, Fall 2026 · Use: course-specific policy

More

Resources I have used for teaching and learning about Chinese languages and language documentation.

Dictionary of Taiwan Hakka

A dictionary of six Taiwan Hakka varieties in traditional Chinese characters (Ministry of Education, Taiwan), for looking up words, standardized characters, and pronunciations.

Free online

Taiwan Hakka Corpus

A corpus of Taiwan Hakka (Hakka Affairs Council), for finding real examples of written Hakka in context.

Free online

Online Dictionary of Indigenous Languages

Dictionaries of 16 Indigenous languages of Taiwan (Council of Indigenous Peoples), for looking up words in each language.

Free online

Chinese Language Resources Protection Project Platform

A database of recordings and data from the national language resources project in Mainland China, for hearing and comparing local varieties of Chinese and minority languages.

Free with registration; requires a Mainland China phone number, and access requirements may change

Great Dictionary of Modern Chinese Dialects

现代汉语方言大词典, a multi-volume series of local Chinese dialect dictionaries (Li Rong, chief ed.), for looking up words and pronunciations in individual local varieties.

Print, through a library; find a copy through WorldCat
